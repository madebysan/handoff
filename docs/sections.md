# Handoff's twelve sections

What each section of the Handoff document covers. The short version is the [README](../README.md).

## The sections

Handoff walks you through twelve sections based on what estate attorneys typically cover with clients:

1. **The Basics:** who you are and why you're creating this
2. **Key Contacts:** family, attorneys, financial advisors
3. **Financial Accounts:** banks, investments, retirement
4. **Insurance Policies:** life, health, property, auto
5. **Property & Assets:** real estate, vehicles, valuables
6. **Digital Life:** device access, emails, passwords, subscriptions, social media
7. **Legal Documents:** will, power of attorney, advance directive
8. **Debts & Liabilities:** mortgages, loans, credit cards
9. **Business Interests:** ownership, partnerships
10. **Dependents & Pets:** care instructions and contacts
11. **Personal Wishes:** funeral, memorial, personal messages
12. **Sign & Finish:** signature, date, personal proof

When you're done, review the document and download a formatted PDF or Markdown file. Nothing is sent to a server. Your data stays in the browser and leaves only when you download the document.
